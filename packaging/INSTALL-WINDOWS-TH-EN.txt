ENGLISH
Quit your DAW. Extract the ZIP and copy the entire Prued FX.vst3 folder into C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3, replacing the previous version. Reopen and rescan. The Mac .command helper is not used on Windows.
Insert on an audio track, play audio and turn Stutter up to capture a loop, back to zero to release it. Fix holds; Yuay refreshes. Dry/Wet blends. Output starts at 0 dB and is adjustable. Saved sessions retain their values.
Free, no key or time limit. TIP / KO-FI opens https://ko-fi.com/yuaylooper only when clicked. Tipping is optional.
